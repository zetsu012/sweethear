VOL. I . . . No. 1ONE HUG

A Message for Priyatna, Delivered from Miles Away

Correspondent Reports on Gratitude, an Apology and a Love That Outlasts the Distance

By
Special to The Birthday Times

, — Happy Birthday my baby ❤️

I honestly do not even know where to begin because there is so much I want to tell you today. There are so many feelings I wish I could express while holding your hands and looking into your eyes instead of typing them through a screen. But since distance is something we have had to live with, I want these words to make you feel just how special you are to me.

First of all baby, I am genuinely so grateful that you were born. I know it sounds like a simple birthday wish, but I mean it with all my heart. I am grateful that the world got someone like you. I am grateful that your parents got to hold you for the first time, that you grew up to become the person you are today and that somehow, among billions of people, our paths crossed. Sometimes I think about how different my life would have been if you had never been born, and I honestly cannot imagine missing out on someone who has become such an important part of my life. Your existence is something I will always be thankful for.

Priyatna Tamang, I am so proud of you, baby. I hope you know that. I am proud of the person you are, your strength, your efforts, your growth and the way you keep going even when life gets difficult. I know there are days when you feel exhausted, misunderstood or disappointed, but I hope you never underestimate yourself. You have so much potential and so many beautiful things waiting for you. I want to see you achieve everything you dream of, become everything you aspire to be and look at yourself one day with the biggest smile because you know how far you have come.

“Your existence is something I will always be thankful for.”

And baby, I also want to apologise for all the times I have hurt you. We both know our relationship has not always been easy. We have had countless arguments, misunderstandings and moments when things could have been handled so much better. I am sorry for every time I made you cry, every night you went to sleep with a heavy heart and every moment when you needed love and reassurance but instead received frustration from me. I am sorry for the things I did knowingly and unknowingly that made you feel sad or unappreciated. I do not want to justify those moments because regardless of my intentions, your tears and feelings were real. You never deserved to go through those moments because of me.

I know saying sorry cannot erase everything, but I genuinely want to become better. I want to be someone who understands you, listens to you and makes you feel secure. I want us to learn from our mistakes rather than keep hurting each other over them.

Despite everything, one thing I have never stopped wanting is to spend time with you. I still remember coming to Kolkata to meet you without telling my parents. The excitement of making that journey just to see you and finally being able to spend time together is something I will always cherish. It was worth every bit of effort because I was getting to see my favourite person.

I also think about our nights together in hotels, when the distance finally disappeared and it was just us. Staying up late, talking, laughing, cuddling, kissing and making love. Having you beside me, feeling your warmth and being able to hold you close instead of missing you through a screen. Those memories mean so much to me. It was never just about the physical intimacy. It was about having you near me, enjoying your presence and feeling that for once I did not have to count the kilometres between us.

And you know what baby? Even after all our fights, misunderstandings and difficult days, getting to spend time with you is still worth it. Every journey, every bit of waiting and every effort feels worth it when I finally get to see you. I want more of those memories. More trips, more random conversations, more hugs, more kisses and more nights where we forget about everything else and simply enjoy being together.

I wish I could be there today to wake you up with kisses, hold you tightly, take you out and make your entire day special. I wish I could see your birthday smile right in front of me instead of through a screen. But until we get to celebrate these days together, I hope you know that someone miles away is incredibly happy that you were born.

Thank you for being you, baby. Thank you for the memories, the affection, the laughter and all the little moments that have made my life more meaningful. I hope this year brings you happiness, success, confidence and everything your heart wishes for. Never stop believing in yourself and never forget how much you deserve to be loved and celebrated.

I am proud of you. I am grateful for you. I am sorry for the times I have hurt you. And I am excited for all the beautiful memories we still have to create together.

Happy Birthday, my Priyatna. I love you so much, baby. And if I could make one birthday wish of my own, it would simply be that I get to celebrate many more of your birthdays, holding you close instead of wishing you from miles away. ❤️

With all my love,
